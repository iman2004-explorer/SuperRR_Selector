THE MODEL

Two lenses. One question.

The site keeps two supplied model versions separate. They use different pressure definitions, scoring approaches, and eligibility rules; compare within a version, not across PRI scales.

VERSION S2RESEARCH MODEL

Expected performance
in context.

Match-grouped cross-fitting estimates expected runs and wickets for each delivery. A player's observed performance is compared with that expectation, then shrunk toward the league average.

  • Chase win probability helps identify contested situations.
  • Separate batter and bowler net value, with wicket value sensitivity.
  • Match-level bootstrap, ranking stability scenarios, and uncertainty.

READ BEFORE YOU RANK

Evidence has edges.

Designed for transparent exploration—not as a substitute for scouting, medical information, auction strategy, or human judgment.

01

Definitions matter.

Pressure thresholds and weights are modelling choices. The two versions intentionally differ and should not be treated as interchangeable.

02

Samples vary.

Some players have much less pressure exposure. S2 reports bootstrap uncertainty and stability indicators; neither removes all uncertainty.

03

Not a forecast.

Historical ball-by-ball data does not account for injuries, current form, role fit, auction price, or future conditions.

04

Check eligibility.

The S2 archive flags Pakistan players as not auction-actionable based on a supplied external assumption. Confirm current rules independently.
